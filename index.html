In plain terms

Old technology goes in. Secure domestic supply comes out.

When an organisation retires servers, laptops, drives or turbines, two things have to happen: the data has to be destroyed beyond recovery, and the materials inside have to be recovered rather than shipped away. Paladin does both, under one contract, and hands you the paperwork that proves it.

For compliance teams

NIST 800-88 destruction, serial-level chain of custody, certificates that survive an audit.

For IT and operations

One vendor, one contract, one report — from pickup through final disposition.

For finance

Recovery revenue offsets disposal cost. Transparent modelling against your actual asset mix.

For the board

Critical materials kept onshore, in allied supply chains, with the audit trail to show it.

Trust is the scarcest commodity in this market.

The recycling and ITAD sector is fragmented and broker-driven, with a reputation for heavy marketing and uneven execution. Paladin is the counter-example: a compliance-led operator built around provable processes, not resale margins or downstream promises.

The problem

Long, opaque hand-offs

Multi-vendor networks and downstream processors create blind spots in security, compliance and material flow.

The problem

Cost stacking and leakage

Fragmented models raise exposure as hyperscale volumes and regulatory scrutiny rise together.

The problem

Unprovable outcomes

Customers cannot show where assets went, how they were processed, or whether data was destroyed.

Chain of custody, secure handling and verifiable recovery are not claims here. They are the operating standard you can audit.

How it works

From pickup to final disposition, inside one system.

01 · Collection

Local intake, standard controls

Hub-and-satellite sites shorten transport routes and apply the same operating model, controls and reporting at every location.

02 · Destruction

Certified, on-site or off

Data-bearing assets are the highest risk in any decommissioning. Destruction is certified and documented at serial level.

03 · Processing

Domestic, not exported

Avoiding broker-led export and resale chains keeps operational control over where assets go and how they are processed.

04 · Recovery

Materials back in circulation

Rare-earth magnets and high-value materials are recovered at usable quality and returned as domestic feedstock.

05 · Proof

One audit trail, end to end

Certificates of destruction and recycling, settlement reporting, asset-level history — available by default, not on request.
