How the work runs

One chain, three checkpoints

Every asset moves through the same sequence wherever it is collected, and each step writes to the same record.

Step one

Collected and scanned

Serial-level capture at the dock, before anything leaves the site. The manifest is generated at the point of pickup, not reconstructed afterwards.

Step two

Destroyed and certified

Data-bearing assets are neutralised first, on site or at a certified facility, and a certificate is issued against each serial number.

Step three

Recovered and reported

Metals, boards and magnets are separated domestically, and settlement is reported against the same record the certificates came from.
