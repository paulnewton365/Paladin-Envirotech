In plain terms

Old technology goes in. Secure domestic supply comes out.

When an organization retires servers, laptops, drives or turbines, two things have to happen: the data has to be destroyed beyond recovery, and the materials inside have to be recovered and kept in domestic supply. Paladin does both, under one contract, and hands you the paperwork that proves it.

For compliance teams

NIST 800-88 destruction, serial-level chain of custody, certificates that survive an audit.

For IT and operations

One vendor, one contract, one report, from pickup through final disposition.

For finance

Recovery revenue offsets disposal cost. Transparent modeling against your actual asset mix.

For the board

Critical materials kept onshore, in allied supply chains, with the audit trail to show it.

Trust is the scarcest commodity in this market.

The recycling and ITAD market is still highly fragmented and broker-driven, with long, opaque hand-offs that make true accountability difficult, and a reputation for heavy marketing and uneven execution. Paladin is the counter-example: a compliance-led operator whose processes can be proven at every step. Recycling only works when it is run as critical infrastructure.

The problem

Long, opaque hand-offs

Multi-vendor networks and downstream processors create blind spots in security, compliance and material flow.

The problem

Cost stacking and leakage

Fragmented models raise exposure as hyperscale volumes and regulatory scrutiny rise together.

The problem

Unprovable outcomes

Customers cannot show where assets went, how they were processed, or whether data was destroyed.

Chain of custody, secure handling and verifiable recovery are not claims here. They are the operating standard you can audit.

One operator

Four companies, four sets of paperwork, nothing that reconciles.

A typical disposition chain passes assets through four or more companies. Each issues its own paperwork, on its own schedule, in its own format, and none of it reconciles. Serial numbers drop out between hand-offs. Paladin owns every stage, so collection, destruction and recovery land on one record.
