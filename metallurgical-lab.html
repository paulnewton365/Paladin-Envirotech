Recycling · Metallurgical lab

Know what is in the material before it is processed

Paladin's IRT analytical lab provides real-time material analysis: what a component actually contains, validated rather than assumed. It is what turns a recycling claim into a number somebody can audit.

What the lab is for

Analysis, not estimation

The same capability serves four different questions, which is why it sits inside the operation rather than at an outside vendor.

Process control

Real-time feedback

Feedback loops for refining, shredding, separation and smelting. Contamination and inefficiency are caught early, which cuts rework.

Risk management

Traceable disposal

Validating recyclability and ensuring e-waste is properly identified and handled, which is what reduces liability on sensitive infrastructure.

Lifecycle insight

Benchmarking across generations

Metal content compared across hardware generations, which informs procurement and refresh cycles rather than just the disposal decision.

Who uses it

Most useful where the hardware is not standard

Proprietary and Open Compute hardware is exactly the case where published recovery assumptions stop being reliable.

Hyperscale data centers

Operators running Open Compute hardware, where metal content differs from anything in a general recycler's reference tables.

Proprietary hardware owners

Anyone whose equipment was built to their own specification and whose recovery figures therefore have to be measured.

Sustainability teams

Validating the recyclability of components and metal content, so circular economy claims rest on analysis.
