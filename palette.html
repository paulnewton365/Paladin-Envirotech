04 — Accents

Each accent needs two values. A graphic tone for squares, rules and large numerals, and a darker or lighter sibling for anything set as small type.

The base tones fail contrast at label size, and the margins are narrow. Cyan measures 4.46 against a 4.5 requirement on the warm ground: it looks right, reads right, and fails.

05 — Balance

43% of bands are dark, down from 55%.

Measured across the site: 48 dark bands against 64 light. Gold reads as premium only while it stays scarce, so the rule is one emphasis phrase per headline plus the eyebrow marker. If an accent appears more than twice in a viewport, remove one.
