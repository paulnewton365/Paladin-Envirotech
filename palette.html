Paladin EnviroTech · color system

Expanded palette and usage

The site currently runs on two colors: one navy and one gold. That is enough for a hero, but not for stat bands, tables, charts, status states, or a light-background page. Below is the same identity widened into a working set of surfaces, text greys and accents, plus semantics.

Kept from current site

Navy 800, Gold 500 and white stay exactly as they are. Nothing below replaces them.

Added

Two darker navies for depth, two accents pulled from the circuit imagery, one recovery green, and three light surfaces — one cool, two warm.

Ratio to hold

55% navy surface, 25% neutral text, 12% gold, 8% everything else. Gold reads as premium only while it stays scarce.

02 — Text and light surfaces

Three text weights on dark, and three light grounds. In application the warm pair replaced the cool one: against this much navy, a cool grey reads as more blue.

White
#FFFFFF

Headlines, nav links, primary button fill. Reserve pure white for the things that must be read first.

Fog 200
#CBD5DE

Body copy on navy. Softer than white at paragraph length, still 11:1 contrast.

Slate 400
#8CA0B3

Captions, stat labels, dates, the EN language toggle. Minimum 14px at this contrast.

Fog 100
#E8EDF2

The original cool light ground. Superseded in application by the warm pair below; keep it for product UI, where cyan lives.

Warm white
#FAF8F5

The upper light ground and card fill. The only light surface small gold text is allowed on.

Warm fog
#EDE9E3

The lower light ground, one step down from warm white, so adjacent light sections stay distinct. Hairlines on both are #DDD6CC.

03 — Accents

Gold carries brand. Cyan carries data. Ember and green carry material states.

Gold
400 #D9A441 · 500 #C08F3C · 600 #9A7130 · 700 #8A6529

One emphasis word per headline, eyebrow markers, stat numerals, the tagline line, link hover. 400 is hover, small type on navy and links on dark; 500 fills the primary button; 600 is gold text at 16px and up on light; 700 is small gold text, and only on warm white. Never a fill larger than a button, and never behind body copy.

Signal cyan
400 #7FD3E3 · 500 #4FB3C8 · 600 #2B7D90

The platform's color. Chain-of-custody timelines, dashboard charts, active tabs, map routes, "verified" ticks. Keeps product UI legible without spending gold on it, and it already exists in the hero photography.

Ember & Recovery green
#C4562C · #3F8F6B

Ember marks critical-materials content and inbound waste streams. Green marks recovered output, tonnage reclaimed, sustainability figures. Together they give the recycling story a before and after without adding a fifth hue.

06 — Rules added in application

What the homepage and Company page settled, now part of the system.

Primary buttons are gold

Gold 500 fill, Navy 800 label, wiping to Gold 400 on hover. White fill is demoted to secondary use. This is the one place gold covers real area.

Category rules are 5px

A coloured top rule codes a card's category: cyan for the system, green for recovered output, ember for inbound and risk, gold for everything else. At 1–2px the colour disappears; 5px is the floor.

Ember and green are graphic only

Squares, rules, ticks and fills. Neither passes small-text contrast on any ground in this system, so neither is ever a text colour.

Small gold needs warm white

Gold 700 clears 4.5:1 on warm white but not on warm fog. Labels under 16px on the fog ground use Steel 500 instead.

Three rules worth writing down: gold never fills an area larger than a button, cyan never appears in marketing headlines or as text on a light ground (it belongs to the product), and any new color must be a tint of one of these hues rather than a new one.
