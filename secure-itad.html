Data-bearing assets are the highest risk in any decommissioning.

Every retired drive, server or device carries data until it is destroyed. Paladin destroys it under NIST 800-88, on-site or off, and documents the result at serial level, before anything moves to recycling or recovery.

Two ways to destroy. One standard behind both.

Where destruction happens is a policy decision. What it has to prove afterwards is not, so the record is identical either way.

On-site 01

Destruction at your location

Mobile shredding and degaussing for assets that can never leave a controlled perimeter.

Off-site 02

Destruction at a certified facility

Chain-of-custody transport into a facility audited to the same standard everywhere Paladin operates.

Standard

NIST 800-88 methods

Clear, purge or destroy, matched to media type and your policy.

01

02
