Platform · Electronics recycling

Processed domestically. Not exported and forgotten.

Once data is destroyed, what remains still has value: ferrous and non-ferrous metals, precious metals, plastics. Paladin processes it domestically under controlled downstream handling, instead of routing it through export and resale chains that lose track of where it ends up.

Metals

Ferrous & non-ferrous

Separated and sorted domestically for direct return into manufacturing supply.

Precious metals

Gold, silver, palladium

Recovered from circuit boards and connectors rather than lost to bulk shredding.

Plastics

Sorted, not landfilled

Housings and casings routed to controlled downstream processors, tracked the same way as everything else.

Control

No unaccountable downstream

Every downstream partner is audited; nothing leaves the record once it enters intake.
