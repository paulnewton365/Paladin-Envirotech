Platform

One system, from pickup to final disposition.

Most ITAD and recycling programs rely on multi-vendor networks, brokers and downstream processors, creating blind spots in security, compliance and material flow. Paladin operates a single integrated system from pickup through destruction, processing and final disposition, removing the hand-offs and shared responsibility that make those blind spots possible.

Why one system

Every hand-off is a blind spot.

A typical disposition chain passes your assets through four or more companies. Each one issues its own paperwork, and none of it reconciles into a single record. Fragmented models increase leakage, cost stacking and exposure, especially as hyperscale volumes and regulatory scrutiny rise together. When an auditor asks what happened to drive serial 7F2-0441, the answer usually depends on who you ask.

The typical chain
Four companies, four sets of paperwork, three points where custody changes hands.
Paladin
One company, one record, zero custody transfers outside the system.
The lifecycle

The audit trail assembles as the assets move.

Each step adds a record to the same file. By the time the last truck leaves, the report your auditor wants already exists, nothing is reconstructed after the fact.
