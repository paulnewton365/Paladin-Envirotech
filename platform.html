Platform

One system, from pickup to final disposition.

Most ITAD programs pass assets through brokers and downstream processors, each with its own controls. Paladin runs one integrated system from pickup to final disposition.

Why one system

Every hand-off is a blind spot.

A typical chain passes assets through four or more companies whose paperwork never reconciles into one record. When an auditor asks what happened to drive serial 7F2-0441, the answer depends on who you ask.

The typical chain
Four companies, four sets of paperwork, three points where custody changes hands.
Paladin
One company, one record, zero custody transfers outside the system.
The lifecycle

The audit trail assembles as the assets move.
