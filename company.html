How we operate

Credibility has to be auditable.

This industry markets heavily and documents lightly. Everything below is something a customer or auditor can check independently, which is the only standard that matters when the assets are already gone.

Certified per site, per entity

R2 and RIOS are issued to a facility and a legal entity, never to a brand. Each acquired company keeps its own certificate until transfer completes, so auditors can verify the exact site handling their assets.

Owned operations, no brokers

Collection, destruction, processing and recovery happen inside the platform. Nothing is handed to a downstream reseller whose controls we cannot show you.

Software built for audit

A proprietary chain-of-custody system records assets at serial level and produces certificates and settlement reporting as the work happens.

Certifications & standards

What we are audited against.

Scope matters as much as the certificate. Activities at non-certified facilities, such as transfer, storage or consolidation, are managed under downstream vendor controls and are stated as outside certification scope.

R2

Responsible Recycling standard for data security, environmental and worker-safety practices in electronics reuse and recycling.

RIOS

Integrated quality, environmental and health-and-safety management system certification for recycling operations.

ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001

Quality, environmental and occupational health-and-safety management systems, held at the sites where they apply.

WEEELABEX

European standard for the collection, logistics and treatment of waste electrical and electronic equipment.

NIST 800-88

Media sanitization guidelines governing how data-bearing assets are destroyed and documented at serial level.

Backing

Institutional capital behind an operating thesis.

SER Capital Partners backs Paladin's expansion and acquisition program, alongside a partnership with Daeheung Recycling in South Korea. The thesis is straightforward: a fragmented, broker-heavy industry consolidates around whoever can prove custody at scale, and the same infrastructure that proves it also recovers the materials the country is short of. Working with hyperscalers, government agencies and enterprises across North America and Europe, Paladin keeps sensitive data protected and high-value materials in circulation.
